#### Description

The PLYRMDEN keyword defines the in situ rock density at reservoir conditions by individual cell, for when the Polymer option has been activated by the [POLYMER](#kw-POLYMER) keyword in the [RUNSPEC](#kw-RUNSPEC) section. PLYRMDEN should consist of an array of real positive values. See the [DENSITY](#kw-DENSITY) parameter on the [PLYROCK](#kw-PLYROCK) keyword in the [PROPS](#kw-PROPS) section for setting the property for the whole grid.
