### FOAMFSO – Define Foam Gas Mobility Reduction versus Oil Saturation


| RUNSPEC | GRID | EDIT | PROPS | REGIONS | SOLUTION | SUMMARY | SCHEDULE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |


#### Description

The FOAMFSO keyword defines the reduction in gas mobility versus oil saturation, for when the Foam option has been activated by the FOAM keyword in the RUNSPEC section, and the FOAMOPT2 parameter on the FOAMOPTS keyword in the PROPS section has been set to the character string FUNC.

This keyword is not supported by OPM Flow but would change the results if supported so the simulation will be stopped.
