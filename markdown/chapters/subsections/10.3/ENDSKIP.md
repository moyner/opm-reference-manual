### ENDSKIP – Deactivate Skipping of Keywords and Input Data {#kw-ENDSKIP}

The ENDSKIP keyword deactivates the skipping of keywords that was activated by the [SKIP](#kw-SKIP), [SKIP100](#kw-SKIP100), or [SKIP300](#kw-SKIP300) keywords. Each [SKIP](#kw-SKIP) keyword should be paired with an ENDSKIP keyword.

See ENDSKIP – Deactivate Skipping of Keywords and Input Data in the GLOBAL section for a full description.
