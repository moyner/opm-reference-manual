### SKIP100 – Activate Skipping of Black-Oil Keywords and Input Data {#kw-SKIP100}

The SKIP100 keyword activates skipping of all keywords and input data by the commercial black-oil simulator until the [ENDSKIP](#kw-ENDSKIP) keyword is encountered. All keywords between the SKIP100 and [ENDSKIP](#kw-ENDSKIP) keywords are ignored by the commercial black-oil simulator. The SKIP100 keyword is ignored by the commercial compositional simulator.

See SKIP100 – Activate Skipping of Keywords by Black-Oil Simulator in the GLOBAL section for a full description.
