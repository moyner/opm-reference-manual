### AQUCT – Define Carter-Tracy Analytical Aquifers {#kw-AQUCT}

The AQUCT keyword defines a Carter Tracy aquifer, the properties of the aquifer, including the Carter-Tracy aquifer influence function associated with the aquifer, defined by the [AQUTAB](#kw-AQUTAB) keyword in the [PROPS](#kw-PROPS) section.

See AQUCT – Define Carter-Tracy Analytical Aquifers in the [GRID](#kw-GRID) section for a full description
