### MULTREGD – Multiply Diffusivities Between Regions {#kw-MULTREGD}

The [MULTREGT](#kw-MULTREGT) keyword multiplies the diffusivity between two regions by a constant. The region number array can be [FLUXNUM](#kw-FLUXNUM), [MULTNUM](#kw-MULTNUM) or [OPERNUM](#kw-OPERNUM) and these arrays must be defined and be available before the [MULTREGT](#kw-MULTREGT) keyword is read by the simulator. The constant should be a real number.

See MULTREGD – Multiply Diffusivities Between Regions in the [GRID](#kw-GRID) section for a full description.
