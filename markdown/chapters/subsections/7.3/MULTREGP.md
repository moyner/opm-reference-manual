### MULTREGP – Multiply Pore Volumes Based On Region Number {#kw-MULTREGP}

The MULTREGP keyword multiplies the pore volume of a cell by a constant for all cells with a specific region number. The region number array can be [FLUXNUM](#kw-FLUXNUM), [MULTNUM](#kw-MULTNUM) or [OPERNUM](#kw-OPERNUM) and these arrays must be defined and be available before the MULTREGP keyword is read by the simulator. The constant should be a real number.

See MULTREGP – Multiply Pore Volumes Based On Region Number in the [GRID](#kw-GRID) section for a full description.
