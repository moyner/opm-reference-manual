### SKIP300 – Activate Skipping of Compositional Keywords and Input Data {#kw-SKIP300}

Turns on skipping of “compositional” keywords until the [ENDSKIP](#kw-ENDSKIP) activation keyword is encountered. All “compositional” keywords between the SKIP300 and [ENDSKIP](#kw-ENDSKIP) keywords are ignored by OPM Flow.
