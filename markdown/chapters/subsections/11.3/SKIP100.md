### SKIP100 – Activate Skipping of Black-Oil Keywords and Input Data {#kw-SKIP100}

This keyword turns on skipping of black-oil keywords until the [ENDSKIP](#kw-ENDSKIP) activation keyword is encountered. All black-oil keywords between the SKIP100 and [ENDSKIP](#kw-ENDSKIP) keywords are ignored by OPM Flow.

See SKIP100 – Activate Skipping of Black-Oil Keywords and Input Data in the GLOBAL section for a full description.
