### OPERNUM – Define Regions for Mathematical Operations on Arrays {#kw-OPERNUM}

This keyword defines the [OPERATE](#kw-OPERATE) region numbers for each grid block. The [OPERATE](#kw-OPERATE) keyword defines  mathematical operations on arrays in the commercial simulator and is currently not supported by OPM Flow.  However, similar functionality is provided by the [ADD](#kw-ADD) and [MULTIPLY](#kw-MULTIPLY) keywords combined with [MULTNUM](#kw-MULTNUM) region array.

See OPERNUM – Define Regions for Mathematical Operations on Arrays in the [GRID](#kw-GRID) section for a full description.
