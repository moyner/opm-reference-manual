### AQUCT – Define Carter-Tracy Analytical Aquifers

The AQUCT keyword defines Carter-Tracy^[Carter, R. D. and Tracy, G. W. “An Improved Method for Calculating Water Influx,” Transactions of AIME (1960) 219, 215-417.] analytical aquifers, the properties of the aquifer, including the Carter-Tracy aquifer influence function associated with the aquifer, defined by the AQUTAB keyword in the PROPS section.

See AQUCT – Define Carter-Tracy Analytical Aquifers in the GRID section.
