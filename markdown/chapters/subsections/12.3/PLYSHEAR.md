### PLYSHEAR – Activate and Define Polymer Shearing Parameters {#kw-PLYSHEAR}

The PLYSHEAR keyword activates and the defines the polymer shear thinning-thickening option for when the polymer option has been activated by the [POLYMER](#kw-POLYMER) keyword in the [RUNSPEC](#kw-RUNSPEC) section.

See PLYSHEAR – Activate and Define Polymer Shearing Parameters in the [PROPS](#kw-PROPS) section for a full description.
