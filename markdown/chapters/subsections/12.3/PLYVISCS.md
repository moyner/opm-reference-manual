### PLYVISCS – Define Polymer-Salt Viscosity Scaling Factors {#kw-PLYVISCS}

PLYSVISCS defines the polymer-salt viscosity scaling factor tables applied to pure water that are used to determine the viscosity of a polymer-salt mixture with respect to increasing polymer saturation within a grid block. The polymer option must be activated by the [POLYMER](#kw-POLYMER) keyword, as well as the brine phase declared by the [BRINE](#kw-BRINE) keyword in the [RUNSPEC](#kw-RUNSPEC) section in order to use this keyword.  However the ECLM keyword in the [RUNSPEC](#kw-RUNSPEC) must not be used with this keyword.

See PLYVISCS – Define Polymer-Salt Viscosity Scaling Factors in the [PROPS](#kw-PROPS) section for a full description.
