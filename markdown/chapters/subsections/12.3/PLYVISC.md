### PLYVISC – Define Polymer Viscosity Scaling Fact {#kw-PLYVISC}

PLYSVISC defines the polymer viscosity scaling factors used to determine the relationship of pure water viscosity with respect to increasing polymer saturation within a grid block. The polymer option must be  activated by the [POLYMER](#kw-POLYMER) keyword in the [RUNSPEC](#kw-RUNSPEC) section in order to use this keyword.

See PLYVISC – Define Polymer Viscosity Scaling Factors in the [PROPS](#kw-PROPS) section for a full description.
