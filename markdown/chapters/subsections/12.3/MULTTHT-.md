### MULTTHT- – Multiply Cell Transmissibility in the -Theta Direction {#kw-MULTTHT-}

MULTTHT- multiples the transmissibility between two cell faces in the -Theta direction for all the cells in the model via an array, that is the keyword sets the transmissibility multiplier of block (I, J, K) between the cells (I, J-1, K) and (I, J, K).  The keyword should only be used with radial grids, as declared by the [RADIAL](#kw-RADIAL) keyword in the [RUNSPEC](#kw-RUNSPEC) section.

See MULTTHT- - Multiply Cell Transmissibility in the -Theta Direction in the [GRID](#kw-GRID) section for a full description.
