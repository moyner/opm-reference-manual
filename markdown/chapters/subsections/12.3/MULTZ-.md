### MULTZ- – Multiply Cell Transmissibility in the -Z Direction

MULTZ- multiples the transmissibility between two cell faces in the -Z direction for all the cells in the model via an array, that is the keyword sets the transmissibility multiplier of block (I, J, K) between the cells (1, J, K-1) and (I, J, K).

See MULTZ- - Multiply Cell Transmissibility in the -Z Direction in the GRID section for a full description.
