### SKIP – Activate Skipping of All Keywords and Input Data {#kw-SKIP}

The SKIP keyword activates skipping of all keywords and input data until the [ENDSKIP](#kw-ENDSKIP) keyword is encountered. All keywords between the SKIP and [ENDSKIP](#kw-ENDSKIP) keywords are ignored.

See SKIP – Activate Skipping of All Keywords and Input Data in the GLOBAL section for a full description.
